AFYA DROP — MVP PLAN
====================

WHAT IT IS
----------
Afya Drop is a clinical decision-support AI assistant for medical personnel in Uganda.
A clinician sends a diagnosis or treatment question over WhatsApp (plain text) and gets
an answer generated STRICTLY from the Uganda Clinical Guidelines (UCG) and any other
reference documents the admins upload — nothing else.

This is a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) product: the AI does not rely on its own
general medical knowledge; it retrieves the relevant UCG passages and answers only from
them. If the UCG does not cover the question, the assistant says so.

There is NO medicine ordering and NO business/sales insight in this product.


LOCKED DECISIONS
----------------
  - Product: clinical decision-support assistant (diagnosis + treatment) for medical personnel.
  - Source of truth: Uganda Clinical Guidelines (UCG) + other files uploaded via the admin panel.
    The assistant answers from these documents ONLY.
  - AI provider: Groq (console.groq.com) — large-language-model inference.
  - Channel: one main WhatsApp business number connected via Baileys (WhatsApp Web). Text only.
  - Access: users register on the website; their WhatsApp number links account <-> wallet.
  - Monetisation: prepaid credits. 1 credit = 100 UGX. Minimum purchase 1,000 UGX = 10 credits.
    Each clinical question costs 1 credit.
  - Payments: PesaPal (UGX).
  - Stack: Supabase (PostgreSQL + pgvector) + Vercel (website/admin) + Railway (backend, AI service, Baileys).
  - DB separation: Option A — the AI microservice NEVER talks to the database. The main backend
    does all Supabase work (retrieval, wallets, users) and passes context to the AI service.


HOW A QUESTION FLOWS (end to end)
---------------------------------
  1. A registered, verified clinician WhatsApps a clinical question to the main number.
  2. The Baileys service receives it and forwards {phone, text} to the main backend.
  3. Backend: look up the user; check they are verified and have >= 1 credit.
  4. Backend: embed the question, run a pgvector similarity search over the UCG chunks,
     and take the top matching passages as context.
  5. Backend: send {question, context} to the AI microservice.
  6. AI microservice (Groq): generate a clinician-structured answer strictly from the context:
       - likely differentials
       - recommended workup / investigations
       - treatment with exact drug doses, route, frequency, duration (adult vs paediatric)
       - red flags / danger signs
       - when to refer
     plus a standing disclaimer: "Decision support only — confirm against the full UCG and
     use your clinical judgement."
  7. Backend: on a successful answer, deduct 1 credit, log the Q&A, and reply on WhatsApp.
     If there is no relevant UCG context, the answer states that the guidelines don't cover it.


SERVICES (Railway)
------------------
  1. backend      — registration/auth, wallets, credits, PesaPal IPN, document ingestion,
                    question routing, pgvector retrieval. The only service that touches Supabase.
  2. ai-service   — Groq clinical answering + embeddings. No DB access (Option A).
  3. baileys      — WhatsApp Web session on the main number; forwards messages to backend and
                    sends replies. Session persisted on a Railway volume.


FRONTEND (Vercel)
-----------------
  - /register  — signup: full name, WhatsApp number (+256), medical qualification (dropdown),
                 practising licence number. OTP is sent over WhatsApp to verify the number.
  - /login     — sign in (MVP: by user id).
  - /dashboard — wallet balance + buy credits (PesaPal checkout; bundles of 10/25/50/100).
  - /admin     — upload the UCG / reference documents; the text is chunked, embedded, and
                 stored for retrieval. List/manage uploaded documents.


DATABASE (Supabase)
-------------------
  users, otp_codes, wallets, credit_transactions, payments,
  documents, document_chunks (vector), qa_logs
  Functions: spend_credit, add_credits, match_document_chunks.
  RLS enabled on all tables; the backend uses the service-role key.


CREDIT RULES (locked)
---------------------
  - 1 credit = 100 UGX.
  - Minimum purchase = 1,000 UGX (10 credits).
  - Each clinical question costs 1 credit.
  - A credit is deducted ONLY after a successful AI answer (a failed Groq call is free).
  - If balance is 0, the user gets a WhatsApp message with a top-up link and no answer.


SAFETY & POSITIONING
--------------------
  - The assistant is a decision-SUPPORT aid for qualified clinicians, not a replacement for
    clinical judgement. Every answer carries the disclaimer.
  - Answers are grounded ONLY in the uploaded UCG/reference text; the model is instructed not
    to use outside knowledge and to say when the guidelines don't cover a question.
  - Recommended: a human review of the UCG text quality and a clinician review of sample answers
    before launch.

DEPLOYMENT PLAN (rollout order)
-------------------------------
  1. Supabase: run schema.sql (tables + pgvector + functions). Enable the vector extension.
  2. Groq: create an API key at console.groq.com. Pick an embeddings model/provider for UCG chunks.
  3. Railway — ai-service: deploy, set GROQ_API_KEY + INTERNAL_API_SECRET + embedding vars, verify /health.
  4. Railway — backend: set Supabase + PesaPal + AI/ Baileys URLs + INTERNAL_API_SECRET, deploy.
  5. Railway — baileys: attach a volume for session state, deploy, scan the QR with the main
     business phone to pair, confirm /health shows connected.
  6. Admin: upload the UCG via /admin, confirm chunks are stored (documents status = ready).
  7. PesaPal: register the IPN URL, run a sandbox 1,000 UGX purchase, confirm 10 credits land.
  8. Vercel: point NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL at the backend and deploy.
  9. End-to-end test:
       - register a clinician (number + qualification + licence) and verify the WhatsApp OTP
       - buy 10 credits via PesaPal sandbox
       - WhatsApp a clinical question (e.g. malaria treatment) -> get a UCG-grounded answer
         and confirm 1 credit is deducted
       - ask something the UCG does not cover -> confirm the assistant says so (no hallucination)
       - check the admin panel lists the uploaded UCG


OUT OF SCOPE (this MVP)
-----------------------
  - Voice notes (text-only over WhatsApp).
  - Medicine ordering / supply, and any business/sales analytics.
  - Multi-currency (UGX only).
  - PDF/DOCX parsing in the admin panel (MVP ingests plain-text exports of the UCG).
